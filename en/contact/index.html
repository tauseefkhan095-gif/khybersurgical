A few helpful answers.

Can I place an order on this website?

No. This is an information and enquiry website. Browse a product, then use the contact form to discuss your requirements with KS.

Where can I find sizes and technical specifications?

Send a product enquiry with the specifications you need. The team must confirm model-specific sizes, materials, packaging and documentation.

Can I enquire about bulk quantities?

Yes. Include your estimated quantity, destination and relevant specifications. Availability, lead times and any commercial terms are confirmed separately.

Which languages can I use?

The catalogue, enquiry form and automated guide are available in English, Arabic, Persian and Turkish. Use the language selector in the header.
